Our Genesis

A platform built out of necessity.

We watched traditional banking systems leave everyday people behind. High-net-worth individuals enjoyed access to global equities, high-yield fixed income, and inflation-hedging tools — but the average person was stuck with savings accounts that barely kept pace with rising costs.

So we built the solution. By leveraging fintech innovation and strategic partnerships with SEC-registered trustees, Novara Capital gives you the exact same institutional-grade investment tools — with a minimum investment starting from just $10.

No complex jargon. No hidden fees. No gatekeeping. Just clear, transparent, and powerful wealth-building instruments, designed for real people with real financial goals.

OUR PRINCIPLES

The values that guide every decision

From product design to investment policy, these are the non-negotiables that make Novara Capital different.

Absolute Security

Your capital is held in segregated accounts by independent licensed custodians. We use bank-grade AES-256 encryption, two-factor authentication, and real-time fraud monitoring on every transaction.

Radical Transparency

No hidden fees, no complex jargon, and absolutely no surprises. Every charge, every rate, and every investment is clearly disclosed before you commit a single dollar.

Relentless Innovation

Markets evolve. Your tools should too. We continuously enhance our platform with AI-driven insights, automated rebalancing, and access to emerging asset classes before they go mainstream.

Client First

Every feature we build, every rate we negotiate, and every policy we write starts with a single question: does this serve our investors better? Your growth is the only metric that matters to us.

Global Reach

Your wealth should not be confined by borders. We give you exposure to S&P 500 companies, Eurobonds, US Treasury Bills, and emerging market assets — all from a single unified account.

Financial Education

Wealth starts with knowledge. Our Novara Academy features curated courses, tools, and market explainers so that every investor — from beginner to advanced — can make confident, informed decisions.

OUR JOURNEY

From an idea to a global platform

2021 — The Beginning

Novara Capital is incorporated

Founded in Lagos with a seed round of $2.5M, we launched our first product: a high-yield savings account offering 4.2% APY — 6x the average bank rate.

22
2022 — First 10,000 Users

Launching the investment platform

We opened access to Nigerian Treasury Bills, Eurobonds, and our Goal Tracker feature. In 8 months, we surpassed $10M in assets under management.

US & Global equities unlocked
