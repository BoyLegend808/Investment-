Security & Asset Protection

We maintain institutional-level custody protocols and regulatory adherence so you can invest with total peace of mind.

FOUR PILLARS OF PROTECTION

How We Protect Your Wealth

1. SEC-Regulated Trustees

All client investments and savings are held in legally isolated trust structures managed by independent, SEC-licensed corporate trustees (Stanbic IBTC Trustees). Crest cannot access or move your capital for operational expenses.

2. Military-Grade 256-Bit SSL

Every interaction and transaction is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / AES-256 bit encryption protocols—the exact standard utilized by leading international financial institutions and sovereign governments.

3. GDPR & Data Privacy

We comply strictly with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and international data privacy guidelines. Your identity documents are verified securely via standard KYC/AML procedures.

4. Tier-1 Depository Custodians

Cash deposits and treasury securities reside in segregated accounts with CBN-licensed commercial depository partner banks and the Central Securities Clearing System (CSCS).

5. Biometric Multi-Factor Auth

Every withdrawal requires two-factor authentication (2FA) via time-based authenticator apps or biometric FaceID / fingerprint verification to prevent unauthorized account access.

6. Vetted Low-Risk Assets

Crest does not engage in speculative crypto trading, uncollateralized lending, or opaque offshore derivatives. We invest strictly in audited sovereign and tier-1 corporate instruments.
