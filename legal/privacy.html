Privacy Policy

How Crest Wealth collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: September 2026

Crest Wealth Ltd ("Crest Wealth", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information when you use our platform:

  • Identity data: First name, last name, date of birth, and government-issued ID details.
  • Contact data: Email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Financial data: Bank account details, investment history, and transaction records.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and usage data.
  • Communications: Emails, chat logs, and support requests you send to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your investment account.
  • Process transactions and send you related information.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations (AML, KYC).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues.
  • Send you product updates and marketing communications (with your consent).
  • Improve and personalise your experience on our platform.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases: contract performance (to deliver investment services), legal obligation (regulatory compliance), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, product improvement), and consent (for marketing communications).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • SEC-registered custodians and licensed trustees who hold your funds.
  • Payment processors and banking partners for transaction processing.
  • Regulatory and law-enforcement authorities when required by law.
  • Service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics) under strict data-processing agreements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, and for up to 7 years after closure to meet regulatory requirements. Marketing data is deleted within 30 days of unsubscription.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format).
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@crestwealth.com.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our platform. You can control cookie preferences via your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures including AES-256 encryption, two-factor authentication, and regular security audits to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer:

  • Email: privacy@crestwealth.com
  • Address: Crest Wealth Ltd, 14 Broad Street, Lagos, Nigeria.
