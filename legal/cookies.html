Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies, session tokens, and local storage to protect your account.

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Crest Wealth Ltd ("Crest Wealth", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies, local browser storage, and related technologies when you visit our website or interact with our client dashboard and investor portal.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you access websites. They allow our platform to recognize your device, maintain active sessions, remember user preferences, and provide bank-grade security during financial operations.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for platform security, fraud prevention, user authentication, and maintaining encrypted sessions while you manage deposits, task validations, or withdrawals.
  • Functionality & Preference Cookies: Save your interface settings (such as dark/light preferences, selected bank presets, and session activity states).
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us measure server response times, page rendering speeds, and identify any navigation friction to continuously optimize your platform experience.

3. LocalStorage & Session Tokens

To ensure high responsiveness and instant client-side ledger synchronization without latency, Crest Wealth securely utilizes browser local storage to maintain verified task credentials, active countdown timers, and transient deposit proofs prior to backend settlement.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to modify cookie settings via their Preferences or Settings menu. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent authentication or restrict access to the Client Dashboard and Admin Observatory.

5. Contact Support

For inquiries regarding our data and cookie handling policies, please reach out to our Compliance Office at privacy@crestwealth.com or via our 24/7 Client Concierge.
